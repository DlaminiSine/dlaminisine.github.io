Bike buyers: an Excel dashboard

Excel project  |  1,000 customers

A screenshot of the dashboard appears on this page when the image file is added to the site.

The question

Which customers are most likely to buy a bike, based on income, gender, commute distance, and age?

  1. 1,000 customers

  4. →

  6. →

What I did

  1. Started from a table of 1,000 customers with marital status, gender, income, children, education, occupation, home ownership, cars, commute distance, region, age, and whether they purchased a bike.
  2. Created an age-bracket column with a nested IF formula: adolescent (under 31), middle age (31 to 54), and old (over 54).
  3. Built pivot tables of average income by gender and purchase, and purchases by commute distance, age bracket, and age.
  4. Assembled a dashboard titled Bike Sales Dashboard with three charts and slicers so the results can be filtered.

Results

  • 481 of the 1,000 customers (48%) bought a bike. Average income was $56,360, and buyers earned slightly more ($57,963) than non-buyers ($54,875).
  • Gender: men averaged $58,063 and women $54,581.
  • Commute distance: customers with a commute of 0 to 1 miles bought at the highest rate (200 of 366, or 55%), while those commuting more than 10 miles bought the least (33 of 111, or 30%).
  • Age: middle-aged customers were the most likely to buy (383 of 701, or 55%), compared with 35% of adolescents and 31% of older customers.

Grouping customers into age brackets

=IF(L2>54, "Old", IF(L2>=31, "Middle Age", IF(L2<31, "Adolescent", "Invalid")))
